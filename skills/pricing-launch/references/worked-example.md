# Pricing Launch Worked Example

## Pricing Strategy
**Model:** Per-user, tiered
**Rationale:** Clear, predictable, scales with team

## Tiers

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | $15/user | Core PM |
| Pro | $25/user | + Time tracking |
| Business | $45/user | + Client portal |

## Launch Offer
- 20% off first year
- 3 months free on annual
- Duration: Launch month only
