# Industry Benchmarks by Stage

## MVP Stage (0-100 users)

### User Engagement

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Weekly active users | Growing | Flat | Declining |
| Activation rate | 40%+ | 20-40% | Below 20% |
| Time to value | Under 5 min | 5-15 min | Over 15 min |

### Product-Market Fit

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| User feedback | Mostly positive | Mixed | Mostly negative |
| Feature requests | Clear patterns | Scattered | No pattern |
| Intent to pay | Expressed | Uncertain | Not expressed |

### Technical Health

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Uptime | 99%+ | 95-99% | Below 95% |
| Error rate | Under 2% | 2-5% | Above 5% |
| Page load | Under 3 sec | 3-5 sec | Over 5 sec |

## How to Use These Benchmarks

1. Identify your stage (MVP, Growth, Scaling)
2. Gather metrics for each category
3. Compare to benchmarks
4. Rate each dimension Green/Yellow/Red
5. Focus on critical gaps first
