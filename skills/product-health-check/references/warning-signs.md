# Warning Signs to Watch

## Red Flags by Category

### User Engagement Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Declining active users | Users dropping off | Investigate why, check onboarding |
| Single feature usage | Users only use one part | Broaden value proposition |
| Shorter sessions | Less engagement | Check for friction or issues |
| No return visits | No ongoing value | Review activation and time-to-value |
| Support requests up | Users confused or frustrated | Review UX, documentation |

---

### Product-Market Fit Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| NPS below 30 | Users not delighted | Deep dive on feedback |
| Churn increasing | Losing customers | Exit interviews, fix issues |
| No referrals | Not enough love | Understand why |
| Feature requests declining | Less interest | Check market fit |
| Competitor wins increasing | Losing positioning | Review differentiation |

---

### Technical Health Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Slow load times | Poor UX, losing users | Performance audit |
| Error rate increasing | Technical debt | Fix bugs, improve stability |
| Downtime incidents | Reliability issues | Infrastructure review |
| Support tickets for bugs | Quality issues | QA improvements |
| Security concerns | Risk of breach | Security audit |

---

### Business Health Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Revenue declining | Business at risk | Review pricing, retention |
| CAC increasing | Acquisition inefficient | Review marketing |
| LTV:CAC below 2:1 | Unit economics broken | Fix pricing or acquisition |
| Burn rate too high | Cash at risk | Cut costs or raise money |
| Gross margin declining | Pricing pressure | Review costs |

---

### Competitive Position Warning Signs

| Warning Sign | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Competitor launches feature | Feature gap | Evaluate priority |
| Losing deals to competitor | Not winning | Sales process review |
| Press about competitor | Mindshare shift | PR/marketing review |
| Customer asks for competitor | Feature gap | Build or partner |
| Industry shift | Market changing | Strategic review |

## Early Warning Signals

### 30-Day Warning Signals

These early signs often predict bigger problems:

1. **Week 1: Drop in sign-ups** - Could indicate acquisition issues
2. **Week 2: Increased support tickets** - Could indicate usability issues
3. **Week 3: Declining engagement** - Could indicate product issues
4. **Week 4: Churn uptick** - Could indicate retention issues

### Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

**Leading Indicators (predict future):**
- Sign-up trends
- Activation rates
- Feature adoption
- NPS scores

**Lagging Indicators (show past):**
- Revenue
- Churn rate
- Retention
- Customer satisfaction

Focus on leading indicators to catch problems early.

## Action Thresholds

### When to Act Immediately

- Any security incident
- Significant downtime
- Rapid revenue decline
- Major competitor win
- Key team member leaves

### When to Monitor

- Minor metric fluctuations
- Single negative feedback
- Small competitor move
- Brief technical issue

### When to Investigate

- Week-over-week decline in any metric
- Two or more warning signs in same category
- Negative feedback pattern emerging

## How to Set Up Early Warning

### 1. Define Your Metrics

Choose 5-7 key metrics that matter for your stage.

### 2. Set Thresholds

Define what "warning" looks like for each:
- Yellow threshold: Start monitoring
- Red threshold: Take action

### 3. Regular Review

- Daily: Critical metrics
- Weekly: All key metrics
- Monthly: Full health check

### 4. Response Plan

For each warning sign:
- Who investigates
- What to investigate
- Timeline for response
