This defines the structure of health check output.

## Quality Guidelines

- Be specific with metrics and numbers
- Provide actionable recommendations
- Connect findings to actions
- Make benchmarks meaningful

## Output Style

Write in plain language. Avoid jargon. Each finding should lead to action.
