# Analyst Relations

Plan and execute analyst relations for B2B growth. This skill creates an analyst strategy.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Analysts
- Target analysts
- Review sites
- Industry coverage

### Step 2: Strategy
- Outreach approach
- Briefing schedule
- Content plan

### Step 3: Review Management
- G2/Capterra presence
- Review solicitation
- Response strategy

### Step 4: Measurement
- Coverage tracking
- ROI assessment

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Analyst Identification
- **Section 2:** Strategy
- **Section 3:** Review Management
- **Section 4:** Measurement

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Target analysts identified
- [ ] Strategy is actionable
- [ ] Review process defined

2. **No ongoing engagement** — One-off briefings.
