# Referral Program Worked Example

## Program Design
**Goal:** 20% of new users from referrals
**Target:** Power users, happy customers

## Incentives
- Referrer: 1 month free (up to 5 users)
- Referred: 20% off first 3 months

## Technical
- Built-in referral tracking
- Unique referral links
- Automatic credit application

## Launch
- Announce at launch
- Email to existing users
- In-app prompt
