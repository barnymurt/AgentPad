# Sales Enablement

Create the materials your sales team needs to sell effectively. This skill produces targeted sales collateral.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Materials Needed
- What does sales need?
- What's the sales process?
- What do prospects ask?

### Step 2: Create Collateral
- Pitch deck
- Battlecards
- Demo script
- FAQ

### Step 3: Competitive Intel
- Competitive comparisons
- Objection responses
- Win themes

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Materials List
- **Section 2:** Pitch Deck Outline
- **Section 3:** Battlecards
- **Section 4:** Competitive Analysis

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Materials match sales process
- [ ] Battlecards are specific
- [ ] Competitive intel is current

## Common Mistakes

1. **Generic materials** — Not tailored to your product.
2. **Outdated intel** — Competitor info goes stale.
3. **No training** — Materials exist but nobody uses them.
