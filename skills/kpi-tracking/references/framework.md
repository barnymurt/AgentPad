# KPI Framework

## Building KPIs

### Step 1: Define Objectives

What are you trying to achieve?

- Grow revenue?
- Acquire users?
- Retain customers?
- Improve engagement?

### Step 2: Choose Metrics

For each objective, pick metrics that measure progress.

### Step 3: Set Targets

What's success?

- Industry benchmarks
- Historical performance
- Growth goals

### Step 4: Track and Report

Regular review and communication.

---

## KPI Selection Criteria

### Must Be

- Aligned with goals
- Measurable
- Actionable

### Nice to Have

- Leading indicator
- Comparability
- Understandable

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too many KPIs** - Focus on 3-5 max
2. **Vanity metrics** - Numbers that look good but don't matter
3. **No targets** - Without targets, no progress
4. **Not reviewing** - KPIs need regular attention
5. **Wrong metrics** - Metrics that don't align with goals
