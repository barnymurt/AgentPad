# KPI Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to validate your KPIs are valuable and actionable.

---

## Strength Test

### 1. Alignment Check

- [ ] Does this KPI connect to a business goal?
- [ ] Can you explain why this matters to the business?
- [ ] Would losing this KPI mean losing visibility into something important?

### 2. Actionability Check

- [ ] If this KPI changes, do you know what to do?
- [ ] Can you influence this KPI with product decisions?
- [ ] Is there a clear response when this KPI declines?

### 3. Measurement Check

- [ ] Can you measure this consistently?
- [ ] Is the data source reliable?
- [ ] Can you get this data in real-time or near real-time?

### 4. Understanding Check

- [ ] Can anyone in the team explain this KPI?
- [ ] Is the calculation transparent?
- [ ] Are there no hidden assumptions?

### 5. Relevance Check

- [ ] Does this KPI matter more than others?
- [ ] Would tracking fewer KPIs be better?
- [ ] Is this a leading or lagging indicator?

---

## Common Problems

### Problem 1: Vanity Metrics

**What it looks like:** "Total users: 10,000" but only 100 are active

**Fix:** Track active users, not just total users

### Problem 2: Lagging Indicators Only

**What it looks like:** Only tracking revenue (which shows past performance)

**Fix:** Add leading indicators (sign-ups, engagement) that predict future revenue

### Problem 3: Too Many KPIs

**What it looks like:** Tracking 20+ metrics, none getting attention

**Fix:** Focus on 5-7 KPIs maximum

### Problem 4: No Targets

**What it looks like:** Tracking "conversion rate: 3%" with no context

**Fix:** Set specific targets based on benchmarks or goals

### Problem 5: Complex Calculations

**What it looks like:** KPI requires 5-step calculation no one understands

**Fix:** Simplify or break into components

---

## Scoring Your KPIs

Rate each KPI 1-5 on:

1. Alignment (1=random, 5=critical)
2. Actionability (1=no action, 5=clear action)
3. Measurement (1=unreliable, 5=real-time)
4. Understanding (1=confusing, 5=crystal clear)
5. Relevance (1=trivial, 5=essential)

**Total: /25**

| Score | Assessment |
|-------|------------|
| 20-25 | Strong KPI - keep |
| 15-19 | Good but improve |
| 10-14 | Weak - reconsider |
| Below 10 | Remove or replace |

---

## Quick Validation Questions

For each KPI, answer:

1. **What decision does this inform?**
   If you can't answer, it's not actionable.

2. **What would we do if it went up?**
   If nothing, it's vanity.

3. **What would we do if it went down?**
   If nothing, it's not important.

4. **How often should we check this?**
   If never, don't track it.
