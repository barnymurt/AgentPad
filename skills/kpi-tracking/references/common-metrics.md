# Common Metrics by Business Model

## SaaS Subscription

| Metric | Definition | Healthy Range |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue | Growing 10%+ MoM |
| Churn | % customers leaving | Below 5% monthly |
| CAC | Customer acquisition cost | LTV:CAC ratio 3:1+ |
| LTV | Lifetime value | $1000+ |
| ARPU | Average revenue per user | Growing |
| NRR | Net revenue retention | 100%+ |

## E-commerce

| Metric | Definition | Healthy Range |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Revenue | Total sales | Growing |
| Conversion | Visitors to buyers | 2-3%+ |
| AOV | Average order value | Stable or growing |
| Repeat purchase | % returning customers | 20%+ |
| CAC | Customer acquisition cost | Below 30% of AOV |

## Marketplace

| Metric | Definition | Healthy Range |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| GMV | Gross merchandise value | Growing |
| Take rate | % of transaction kept | 10-30% |
| Match rate | Successful transactions | 50%+ |
| Supply/demand | Balance | Stable |
| Unit economics | Per-transaction profit | Positive |

## Mobile App

| Metric | Definition | Healthy Range |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| DAU | Daily active users | Growing |
| DAU/MAU | Engagement ratio | 20%+ |
| Retention D1 | Day 1 retention | 40%+ |
| Retention D30 | Day 30 retention | 10%+ |
| ARPDAU | Revenue per daily user | Growing |
