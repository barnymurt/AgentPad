# KPI Tracking Output Schema

This defines the structure of KPI tracking output.

## Output Structure

```
KPI FRAMEWORK

Business Objective: [Goal]
Timeframe: [Q1 2026, etc.]

---

KPI DEFINITIONS

KPI 1: [Name]
Definition: [How it's calculated]
Target: [Number]
Current: [Number]
Progress: [X%]
Data Source: [Where it comes from]

KPI 2: ...

---

TARGETS AND PROGRESS

| KPI | Current | Target | Progress | Trend |
|-----|---------|--------|----------|-------|
| [Name] | [X] | [Y] | [Z%] | Up/Down/Flat |

---

TRACKING PLAN

Frequency: [Weekly/Monthly]
Method: [Dashboard, report, etc.]
Owner: [Who tracks]
Review: [When]

---

REPORTING

Format: [Dashboard/Email/Slides]
Audience: [Who receives]
Frequency: [Weekly/Monthly]
Key Points to Communicate:
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]

---

NOTES

[Assumptions]
[Open questions]
[Next steps]
```

## Quality Guidelines

- Make KPIs actionable
- Include specific targets
- Show progress clearly
- Define owner and frequency
