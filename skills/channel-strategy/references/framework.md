## Prioritization Framework

### RACE Model
- **R**each: Build awareness
- **A**ct: Drive engagement
- **C**onvert: Get customers
- **E**ngage: Retain customers

### Selection Criteria
1. Can we reach our audience?
2. Do we have resources?
3. Can we measure results?
4. Is it sustainable?

## Budget Allocation
