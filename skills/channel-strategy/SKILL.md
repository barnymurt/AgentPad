# Channel Strategy

Select and prioritize the marketing channels that will most effectively reach your target audience. This skill moves beyond "do SEO and social media" to a specific, data-informed channel strategy.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Audit Current Channels

- What channels exist?
- What's working/not working?
- What's the team capacity?

### Step 2: Evaluate Channel Options

- List potential channels
- Assess fit for your audience
- Estimate cost and effort

### Step 3: Prioritize Channels

- Score channels on potential impact
- Consider resource requirements
- Select top 3-5 for focus

### Step 4: Allocate Budget

- How to split resources?
- What's the testing budget?
- What's the scaling budget?

### Step 5: Define Testing Plan

- How to validate channel works?
- What metrics to track?
- What's the test timeline?

## Output Format

- [ ] Budget allocation is realistic

## Common Mistakes

1. **Doing everything** — Spreading thin, nothing works well. Focus on 2-3 channels.
2. **Ignoring resources** — Choosing channels you can't sustain.
3. **No testing** — Assuming channels work without validating.
