# Launch Analytics

Define what to measure during your launch and how to track it. This skill ensures you have the data to understand what's working and make informed decisions.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Define Launch Goals

- What does success look like?
- Revenue targets?
- User acquisition goals?
- Engagement goals?

### Step 2: Identify Key Metrics

- Primary metrics (North Star)
- Secondary metrics (supporting)
- Guardrail metrics (what to monitor)

### Step 3: Plan Tracking

- What tools?
- What events to track?
- Attribution model?

### Step 4: Design Dashboard

- What to show?
- Who sees what?
- Update frequency?

### Step 5: Define Reporting

- Who needs reports?
- How often?
- What format?

## Common Mistakes

1. **Tracking everything** — Data overload. Focus on key metrics.
2. **No baseline** — Can't measure improvement without knowing where you started.
3. **No action on data** — Collecting data is useless if nobody looks at it.
