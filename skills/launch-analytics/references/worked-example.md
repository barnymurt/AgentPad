# Launch Analytics Worked Example

## Context

**Product:** TaskFlow — Project management for creative agencies
**Launch:** Full public launch

---

## Launch Goals

**Success definition:** Get 100 paying customers in first 90 days

**Targets:**
- 1,000 signups (10% conversion to paid)
- 500 email list signups
- 10,000 website visits

---

## Key Metrics

### North Star
- **Paid customers** — Total paying customers

### Supporting Metrics
- Signups (top of funnel)
- Activation rate (completed onboarding)
- Trial-to-paid conversion
- Revenue

### Guardrail Metrics
- Churn rate
- Support tickets
- NPS

---

## Tracking Plan

### Tools
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4
- **Product:** Mixpanel
- **Email:** ConvertKit

### Events to Track
- Page views (all)
- Signup (button click)
- Onboarding completed
- First project created
- Trial started
- Payment succeeded

### Attribution
- UTM parameters
- First-touch model

---

## Dashboard

### Launch Overview Dashboard
- Daily signups
- Daily trials
- Daily paid
- Revenue (MTD)
- Top channels

---

## Reporting

- **Daily:** Launch team (Slack)
- **Weekly:** Full team (email)
- **Monthly:** Leadership (meeting)
