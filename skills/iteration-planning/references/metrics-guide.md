# Metrics Guide: What to Track at Each Stage

## Product Stages and Relevant Metrics

### Stage 1: Pre-Launch / MVP

**Focus:** Validation - does anyone want this?

| Metric | What It Tells You | How to Measure |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Intent sign-ups | Interest level | Email sign-ups, waitlist |
| Feature interest | What they want | Surveys, interview questions |
| Problem urgency | Is this a real pain? | Interview intensity of complaints |
| Willingness to pay | Business model validation | Survey: "Would you pay $X?" |

**Don't worry about:** Retention, engagement, conversion rates yet.

---

### Stage 2: Early Launch (0-100 users)

**Focus:** Product-market fit signals

| Metric | What It Tells You | Benchmark |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| Weekly active users | Core value delivered | Growing week over week |
| Activation rate | Can users get value? | 40%+ should activate |
| User feedback themes | What needs fixing | Track recurring issues |
| Time to value | How fast users get value | Aim for <5 minutes |

**Key question:** Are the right users finding value?

---

### Stage 3: Product-Market Fit (100-1000 users)

**Focus:** Scaling what works

| Metric | What It Tells You | Benchmark |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| Retention curve | Are users staying? | Flattening at 20%+ after 30 days |
| Monthly recurring revenue | Business viability | Growing month over month |
| Net Promoter Score | User love | 50+ is excellent |
| Feature usage distribution | What's actually used | 80/20 rule: 20% of features get 80% of use |

---

### Stage 4: Growth (1000-10,000+ users)

**Focus:** Optimization and efficiency

| Metric | What It Tells You | Benchmark |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| Customer acquisition cost | Marketing efficiency | LTV:CAC ratio > 3:1 |
| Customer lifetime value | Long-term value | Growing over time |
| Churn rate | Retention health | <5% monthly for SMB |
| Expansion revenue | Upsell success | Growing % of revenue |
| Support ticket rate | Product stability | Decreasing over time |

## Metric Prioritization by Business Model

### SaaS Subscription

| Priority | Metrics |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | MRR, Churn, CAC, LTV |
| 2 | Activation rate, Expansion revenue |
| 3 | NPS, Support tickets |

| Priority | Metrics |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | GMV, Take rate, Match rate |

## Common Metric Mistakes

### 1. Tracking Everything

Problem: Data overload leads to analysis paralysis.

Solution: Focus on 3-5 metrics max. Everything else is noise.

### 2. Vanity Metrics

Problem: Metrics that look good but don't matter.

Examples:
- Total users (vs. active users)
- Page views (vs. meaningful engagement)
- Sign-ups (vs. activated users)

Solution: Ask "What decision does this metric inform?"

### 3. Lagging Indicators Only

Problem: Only measuring after the fact.

Solution: Add leading indicators:
- Lead conversion (predicts revenue)
- Feature adoption (predicts retention)
- Support sentiment (predicts churn)

### 4. No Benchmarks

Problem: Don't know if numbers are good.

Solution:
- Industry benchmarks
- Historical comparison
- Cohort comparison

## What to Do If You Don't Have Data

### Start Simple

1. **Google Analytics** - Free, covers basics
2. **Product analytics** - Mixpanel, Amplitude, or PostHog (many have free tiers)
3. **Spreadsheet tracking** - Manual is okay to start

### Minimum Viable Tracking

For early stage, track:

1. **Who is using the product?** (User data)
2. **What are they doing?** (Action data)
3. **Are they coming back?** (Retention data)

You can start with a simple spreadsheet before investing in tools.

### Qualitative Over Quantitative

Early stage, qualitative data is often more valuable:

- 10 user interviews > 1000 survey responses
- Watch users use your product
- Talk to customers directly

## Actionable Metrics Framework

For each metric, ask:

1. **What decision does this inform?**
2. **What would we do if it went up?**
3. **What would we do if it went down?**
4. **What is a good target?**

If you can't answer these, the metric isn't actionable.
