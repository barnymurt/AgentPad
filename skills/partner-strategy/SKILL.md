# Partner Strategy

Develop a strategy for partnerships that accelerate growth. This skill identifies and plans strategic partnerships.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Define Partnership Goals
- What do partnerships achieve?
- What kind of partners?
- What's the business model?

### Step 2: Identify Partners
- Potential partners list
- Fit assessment
- Priority ranking

### Step 3: Outreach Strategy
- Approach
- Value proposition
- Terms

### Step 4: Partnership Management
- Onboarding
- Success metrics
- Communication

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Partnership Goals
- **Section 2:** Partner Identification
- **Section 3:** Outreach Strategy
- **Section 4:** Management Plan

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Partners align with goals
- [ ] Value exchange is clear
- [ ] Management is defined

## Common Mistakes

1. **Wrong partners** — Not aligned with target customer.
2. **No value for partner** — Why would they partner?
3. **No process** — Partnerships fizzle out.
