# Product OKRs Output Schema

This defines the structure of OKR output.

## Output Structure

```
PRODUCT OKRS

Quarter: [Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4] [Year]

---

OBJECTIVE 1: [Objective statement]

Why this matters: [1-2 sentences]

Key Result 1: [KR statement]
Current: [X]
Target: [Y]
Progress: [X%]
How we measure: [Method]

Key Result 2: [KR statement]
Current: [X]
Target: [Y]
Progress: [X%]
How we measure: [Method]

Key Result 3: ...

---

OBJECTIVE 2: [Objective statement]

[Same structure]

---

OBJECTIVE 3: ...

---

CROSS-TEAM DEPENDENCIES

What We Need from Others:
- Team: [What]
- Team: [What]

What Others Need from Us:
- Team: [What]
- Team: [What]

---

RISKS

Risk 1: [Description]
Mitigation: [How to address]
Owner: [Who]

Risk 2: ...

---

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Company: [Name]
Their Objective: [What they set]
Key Results: [Their KRs]
Outcome: [What happened]
Lesson: [What we can learn]

---

NOTES

[Assumptions]
[Open questions]
[Next steps]
```

## Quality Guidelines

- Make objectives inspiring but specific
- Include specific numbers for KRs
- Show current vs target
- Connect to company goals
- Include real-world example
