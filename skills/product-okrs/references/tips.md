# Setting Effective OKRs

## Tips for Success

### 1. Start with Why

Connect OKRs to bigger purpose.

### 2. Limit Number

- Maximum 3-5 objectives per team
- 2-5 key results per objective
- Focus is key

### 3. Think Outcomes, Not Outputs

**Output:** Ship 10 features
**Outcome:** Increase activation rate by 30%

### 4. Make It Measurable

Use specific numbers and percentages.

### 5. Review Regularly

- Weekly: Quick check
- Monthly: Progress update
- Quarterly: Full review

### 6. Set Stretch Goals

70% achievement = good. 100% = wasn't ambitious enough.

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Too many** - Focus is lost
2. **Output-focused** - Not outcomes
3. **No stretch** - Too easy or too hard
4. **Not tracked** - Forget until end of quarter
5. **Not aligned** - Don't connect to company goals
