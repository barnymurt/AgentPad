# Product OKRs

Set and manage objectives and key results for your product. Align teams around measurable outcomes.

## When to Use This Skill

Use when:
- Setting quarterly goals
- Planning next quarter
- Aligning team around outcomes
- Tracking progress
- Reviewing OKR performance

## OKR Basics

### Structure

**Objective:** What you want to achieve
- Qualitative
- Inspiring
- Ambitious

**Key Results:** How you'll measure success
- Quantitative
- Measurable
- Time-bound

### Common Mistake

Bad OKR: "Launch feature X"
Good OKR: "Objective: Improve user activation. KR: Increase activation rate from 30% to 50%"

---

## Output Format

The skill produces these sections:

**QUARTERLY OKRS**

For each objective:
- Objective statement
- Key results with targets
- Progress tracking

**REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE**

Company and their OKR approach

**CROSS-TEAM DEPENDENCIES**

What others need from us
What we need from others

**RISKS**

Risk and mitigation
