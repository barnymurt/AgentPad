# Content Strategy

Create a strategic content plan that attracts and engages your target audience. Unlike random blog posts, this skill produces a cohesive content strategy aligned with your product, audience, and business goals.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Define Content Goals

- What do you want content to achieve?
- Awareness, leads, engagement, retention?
- How will you measure success?

### Step 2: Know Your Audience

- Who are you writing for?
- What do they care about?
- Where do they consume content?

### Step 3: Develop Content Themes

- 3-5 core themes that align with product value
- Topics that attract your target audience
- Balance between promotional and value content

### Step 4: Choose Content Formats

- Blog posts, guides, case studies, videos
- Match format to audience preference
- Consider production capacity

### Step 5: Plan Distribution

- Where will content live?
- How will people find it?
- Social, SEO, email, paid?

### Step 6: Build Content Calendar

- Timeline for content production
- Publishing cadence
- Owner assignments

- [ ] Goals are specific and measurable
- [ ] Content themes align with product value
- [ ] Formats match audience preferences
- [ ] Calendar is realistic given resources
- [ ] Distribution strategy is practical

## Common Mistakes

1. **Writing about anything** — No theme, no strategy. Pick themes and stick to them.
2. **Promotional only** — People tune out constant selling. Lead with value.
3. **No distribution** — Great content nobody sees. Plan promotion.
4. **Unrealistic cadence** — Committing to daily posts, burning out.
