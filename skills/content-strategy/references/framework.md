# Content Strategy Framework

## Content Goals

| Goal | Description | Metrics |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Awareness | Get product known | Reach, impressions |
| Leads | Generate prospects | Signups, MQLs |
| Engagement | Keep audience | Time on site, return visits |
| Retention | Keep customers | NPS, retention rate |

## Audience Research

### Research Methods
- Customer interviews
- Surveys
- Social listening
- Competitor audience analysis

### Audience Questions
- What do they search for?
- What problems do they have?
- What content do they consume?
- What channels do they use?

## Content Themes

### Theme Development
1. Align with product value
2. Address audience pain points
3. Different from competitors
4. Sustainable to produce

### Theme Examples for SaaS
- How-to guides
- Industry trends
- Product tips
- Case studies
- Thought leadership

## Content Formats

| Format | Best For | Effort |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Blog posts | SEO, education | Medium |
| Guides | Deep content | High |
| Case studies | Credibility | High |
| Videos | Engagement | High |
| Infographics | Sharing | Medium |
| Podcasts | Authority | High |

## Distribution Strategy

### Owned Channels
- Website/blog
- Email list
- Product

### Earned Channels
- Social shares
- Press/coverage
- Backlinks

### Paid Channels
- Social ads
- Search ads
- Sponsored content

## Content Calendar

### Cadence Options
- Daily: Ambitious, requires team
- 2-3x/week: Sustainable for small team
- Weekly: Good starting point
- Bi-weekly: Minimum for SEO

### Calendar Structure
- Topic
- Format
- Channel
- Author
- Due date
