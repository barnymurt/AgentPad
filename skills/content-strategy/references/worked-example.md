# Content Strategy Worked Example

## Context

**Product:** TaskFlow — Project management for creative agencies
**Goal:** Build awareness and generate leads

---

## Content Goals

**Primary:** Generate signups from creative agency owners
**Secondary:** Build brand awareness in creative industry

**Success metrics:**
- Monthly blog traffic: 2,000 visits
- Email signups: 100/month

---

## Audience

**Primary audience:** Creative agency owners (5-50 employees)
- Struggling with project management
- Using Asana/Monday but not loving it
- Active on Twitter, LinkedIn

**Content needs:**
- Time-saving tips
- Industry trends
- Productivity hacks

---

## Content Themes

### Theme 1: Project Management for Creatives
- "How creative agencies manage projects"
- "Project management mistakes to avoid"
- "Best tools for creative teams"

### Theme 2: Time Tracking & Invoicing
- "How to bill more accurately"
- "Time tracking for agencies"
- "Invoicing best practices"

### Theme 3: Client Communication
- "Client feedback tools"
- "Managing client expectations"
- "How to handle scope creep"

---

## Format Strategy

| Format | Cadence | Purpose |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Blog posts | Weekly | SEO, education |
| Guides | Monthly | Lead magnets |
| Case studies | Quarterly | Credibility |
| Social | Daily | Engagement |

---

## Distribution

- Blog (owned)
- Twitter (earned/owned)
- LinkedIn (earned)
- Email newsletter (owned)
