# Devil's Advocate (GTM)

Challenge your go-to-market strategy to find weaknesses before launch. This skill stress-tests your GTM.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Assumptions
- List all GTM assumptions
- Categorize by type

### Step 2: Challenge Each
- What could go wrong?
- What's being ignored?
- What's the counter-evidence?

### Step 3: Find Blind Spots
- What's missing?
- What's underestimated?
- What's overestimated?

### Step 4: Recommend
- Priority fixes
- Risk acceptance
- Trade-offs

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Assumption Register
- **Section 2:** Challenge Findings
- **Section 3:** Blind Spots
- **Section 4:** Recommendations

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Assumptions are specific
- [ ] Challenges are rigorous
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable

## Common Mistakes

1. **Surface-level challenges** — Not digging deep enough.
2. **No action** — Finding issues without fixes.
3. **Bias** — Going easy on your own strategy.
