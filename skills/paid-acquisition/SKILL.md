# Paid Acquisition

Plan and execute paid acquisition channels to drive traffic and conversions. This skill provides a structured approach to paid marketing.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Select Channels
- Evaluate Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, etc.
- Match channels to audience

### Step 2: Define Campaigns
- Campaign objectives
- Audience targeting
- Budget allocation

### Step 3: Creative Requirements
- Ad formats needed
- Copy guidelines
- Creative brief

### Step 4: Setup Plan
- Platform setup
- Tracking
- Attribution

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Channel Selection
- **Section 2:** Campaign Strategy
- **Section 3:** Creative Requirements
- **Section 4:** Setup Plan

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Channels match audience
- [ ] Campaigns have clear objectives
- [ ] Budget is realistic

## Common Mistakes

1. **No testing budget** — Need to test before scaling.
2. **Wrong channel** — Not where your audience is.
3. **No tracking** — Can't optimize without data.
