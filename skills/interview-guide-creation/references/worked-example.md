# Worked Example: Interview Guide Creation

This file shows the Interview Guide Creation skill applied to a complete scenario.

---

## Scenario: InvoiceFlow - Freelancer Payment Pain Points

**Context:** InvoiceFlow wants to understand how freelancers manage invoices and get paid. They need an interview guide to conduct discovery research with their target users: freelance designers and developers who currently use manual processes or basic tools.

---

## Output: Interview Guide

# Interview Guide: Freelancer Payment Pain Points

## 1. Research Overview

### Research Type
- [x] Discovery
- [ ] Validation
- [ ] Feedback
- [ ] Evaluation

### Research Objectives

1. Understand freelancer invoice management workflows
2. Identify pain points in getting paid
3. Learn what would make payment process easier
4. Discover tools and methods currently used

### Target Participants
- Role: Freelance designers and developers
- Experience: 1+ years freelancing
- Company size: Solopreneur to 3-person team

## 2. Participant Criteria

### Screener Questions

| # | Question | Yes Criteria | No Criteria |
|---|----------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | Do you do freelance work (design, development, consulting)? | Yes → Continue | Thank and close |
| 2 | How long have you been freelancing? | 1+ years → Continue | Thank and close |
| 3 | Do you invoice clients for your work? | Yes → Continue | Skip to Q4 |
| 4 | Do you send invoices monthly or more frequently? | Yes → Continue | Thank and close (infrequent) |
| 5 | Are you involved in choosing how you get paid? | Yes → Continue | Thank and close |
| 6 | Are you available for a 45-minute call this week? | Yes → Continue | Schedule later |

### Recruitment Sources
- [x] Existing customers (InvoiceFlow user list)
- [x] LinkedIn outreach (freelancer groups)
- [x] Professional networks (designer/developer Slack communities)
- [ ] Recruitment platform
- [ ] Other: Cold outreach via Twitter

### Target Participants
- Number: 6-8 participants
- Duration: 45 minutes per interview

---

## 3. Interview Structure

### Timing

| Section | Time | Purpose |
|---------|------|---------|
| Opening | 3 min | Welcome, purpose, consent |
| Warm-up | 5 min | Build rapport, background |
| Core Questions | 25 min | Main research |
| Deep-dive | 10 min | Priority topics |
| Wrap-up | 2 min | Summary, thank you |

**Total:** 45 minutes

---

## 4. Screener Script

```
Hi [Name], thanks for your interest in our research about freelancer payment experiences. This will only take 2-3 minutes.

1. Do you do freelance work - like design, development, or consulting - for clients?
   - Yes → Continue
   - No → Thank and close

2. How long have you been doing freelance work?
   - Less than 1 year → Note (not qualified, but keep warm)
   - 1+ years → Continue

3. Do you send invoices to your clients for payment?
   - Yes → Continue
   - No → Skip to Q4

4. About how often do you send invoices?
   - Monthly or more frequently → Continue
   - Less than monthly → Thank and close (infrequent)

5. Are you personally involved in choosing how you get paid (vs. client deciding)?
   - Yes → Continue
   - No → Thank and close

6. Great! I'd love to chat. Are you available for a 45-minute call this week?
   - Yes → Get email for calendar
   - No → Ask about availability

Thanks so much! We'll be in touch soon.
```

## 5. Interview Questions

### Opening Script

```
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. 

My name is [Name] and I'm researching how freelancers handle getting paid. We're trying to understand the experience better to build better tools.

This will take about 45 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers - I just want to hear about your honest experiences. 

With your permission, I'll record this for my notes, but nothing will be shared publicly. 

Let's get started!
```

### Warm-up Questions

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| Q1 | Tell me a bit about your freelance work. What do you do? | Context - understand role |
| Q2 | How long have you been freelancing? | Experience level |
| Q3 | What does a typical week look like for you? | Workflow context |

### Core Questions

| # | Question | Objective | Follow-ups |
|---|----------|-----------|------------|
| Q4 | Walk me through how you send invoices to clients. | Current workflow | What tools? How do you track? |
| Q5 | What does getting paid typically look like? | Payment process | How long? Any issues? |
| Q6 | Tell me about a time when payment was difficult or delayed. | Pain points | What happened? How did you feel? |
| Q7 | What tools or methods do you use to manage invoices? | Current solutions | Pros/cons? Anything missing? |
| Q8 | What would make getting paid easier? | Opportunities | If you could change anything? |
| Q9 | How do you decide when to follow up on unpaid invoices? | Process | When? How? What works? |
| Q10 | Have you ever lost money due to invoice issues? | Severity | What happened? How much? |
| Q11 | What happens if a client doesn't pay? | Risk handling | What do you do? |
| Q12 | How do different types of clients (one-time vs retainer) affect how you invoice? | Segmentation | Differences? |

### Deep-dive Questions

| # | Question | Why Important |
|---|----------|---------------|
| Q13 | If you could design your ideal payment experience, what would it look like? | Dream state - key insights |
| Q14 | What would make you switch from your current method to something new? | Switching barriers |

### Wrap-up Script

```
That's all my questions. Is there anything else you'd like to share that we haven't covered?

Thank you so much for your time and honesty. Your insights are really helpful for understanding the freelancer payment experience.

[If recording:] I'll send a summary in the next few days. 

Any questions for me?
```

## 6. Follow-up Probes

### Standard Probes

| Probe | When to Use | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Silence | After answer, wait | [Wait 5 seconds - they'll often continue] |
| "Tell me more" | Want depth | "Can you tell me more about that?" |
| Example | Need specifics | "Can you give me a concrete example of that?" |
| Why | Understand reason | "Why is that important to you?" |
| How | Understand process | "How do you typically handle that situation?" |
| Feel | Explore emotions | "How did that make you feel?" |
| Compare | Context | "How is that different from what you used to do?" |

### Probing Best Practices
- Don't rush through silences
- Follow interesting answers, not just script
- Get specific examples
- Explore contradictions ("You said X, but also Y - help me understand")

## 7. Interviewer Tips

### Before Interview
- [x] Review guide thoroughly
- [x] Test recording setup
- [x] Prepare notes template
- [x] Have water ready
- [x] Prepare participant name/background notes

### During Interview
- [x] Be conversational - this is a chat, not interrogation
- [x] Build rapport - share briefly about yourself too
- [x] Listen more than talk
- [x] Take notes on interesting points
- [x] Watch for non-verbal cues (frustration, excitement)
- [x] Stay on time - don't go too long

### After Interview
- [x] Record notes immediately while fresh
- [x] Fill in gaps within 2 hours
- [x] Note follow-up needed
- [x] Send thank you email within 24 hours

## 8. Pilot Plan

### Recommended Pilot
- **Participants:** 2 people - 1 designer, 1 developer (or colleagues)
- **Purpose:** Test questions, timing, flow
- **Adjust based on pilot:**
  - [ ] Remove confusing questions
  - [ ] Add missing topics
  - [ ] Adjust timing

### Pilot Red Flags to Watch
- Questions misunderstood consistently
- Interview runs way over/under time
- Awkward transitions between sections
- Missing pain points we expected to hear about
