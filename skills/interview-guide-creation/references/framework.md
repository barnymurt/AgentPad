# Framework: Interview Guide Creation

This file provides detailed methodology for creating effective research interview guides.

## 1. Research Objective Types

### Discovery Research
**Purpose:** Learn about user problems, behaviors, needs

**When to use:**
- Starting from scratch
- Understanding new market
- Learning about user workflows

**Example objectives:**
- Understand how freelancers manage invoices
- Learn what makes project management frustrating

### Validation Research
**Purpose:** Test assumptions, concepts, or solutions

**When to use:**
- Have hypotheses to test
- Concept to evaluate
- Solution to assess

**Example objectives:**
- Validate that automatic reminders reduce missed payments
- Test appeal of new pricing tiers

### Feedback Research
**Purpose:** Gather input on existing product or prototype

**When to use:**
- Usability testing
- Feature feedback
- Satisfaction assessment

**Example objectives:**
- Get feedback on new dashboard design
- Understand feature usage patterns

## 2. Question Types and Examples

### Open-Ended Questions
**Purpose:** Explore broadly

```
"Tell me about..."
"Describe your experience with..."
"What does your typical day look like?"
```

### Specific Questions
**Purpose:** Get detailed information

```
"What tools do you currently use?"
"Can you walk me through how you..."
```

### Comparative Questions
**Purpose:** Understand context and alternatives

```
"How is this different from what you used before?"
"What other solutions have you tried?"
```

### Behavioral Questions
**Purpose:** Understand past actions

```
"Tell me about a time when..."
"When was the last time you..."
```

### Hypothetical Questions
**Purpose:** Explore possibilities

```
"If you could have anything..."
"What would happen if..."
```

## 3. Question Sequencing

### Opening (2-3 minutes)
- Thank participant
- Purpose of interview
- How long
- What will happen

### Warm-up (5 minutes)
- Easy questions
- Role and background
- Build rapport

### Core (20-30 minutes)
- Main research questions
- Most important topics
- Open-ended exploration

### Deep-dive (10 minutes)
- Most critical areas
- Specific examples
- Detailed exploration

### Wrap-up (5 minutes)
- Summary
- Any final thoughts
- Thank you, next steps

---

## 4. Probe Techniques

### Silence
Wait 3-5 seconds after participant answers. They often continue with more detail.

### Echo
Repeat or reflect back what they said:
"So you found that..."

### Specific
Get more detail:
"What did that look like?"
"What happened next?"

### Example
Request concrete instance:
"Can you give me a specific example?"

### Why
Understand reasoning:
"Why do you think that happened?"
"Why is that important?"

### How
Understand process:
"How do you typically handle that?"
"How would you describe the difference?"

### Feel
Explore emotions:
"How did that make you feel?"
"What was your reaction?"

## 5. Screener Question Template

```
## Screener: [Research Topic]

### Qualification Questions

1. [Screen for target role]
   - Question: _________?
   - Criteria: _________?

2. [Screen for experience]
   - Question: _________?
   - Criteria: _________?

3. [Screen for involvement]
   - Question: _________?
   - Criteria: _________?

### Logistics

4. Availability
   - Question: Are you available for a 45-minute call [date/time]?
   - Yes → Continue

5. Recording consent
   - Question: May we record this interview for research purposes?
   - Yes → Continue

### Contact

6. Information
   - What's your email for the calendar invite?
```

## 6. Interviewer Facilitation Tips

### Before Interview
- Review guide thoroughly
- Prepare recording setup
- Test technology
- Have water, notes ready

### During Interview
- Be conversational
- Make participant comfortable
- Listen more than talk
- Take notes on interesting points
- Watch for non-verbal cues

### After Interview
- Record notes immediately
- Fill in gaps while fresh
- Note follow-up needed
- Send thank you

## 7. Pilot Testing Guide

### Why Pilot Test
- Practice delivery
- Time questions
- Identify confusing questions
- Refine flow

### How to Pilot
1. Recruit 1-2 people similar to target participants
2. Run full interview
3. Note:
   - Questions that don't work
   - Time per section
   - Flow issues
4. Revise guide

### Pilot Red Flags
- Questions consistently misunderstood
- Interview runs too long/short
- Awkward transitions
- Missing key topics

## 8. Common Question Mistakes

### Don't Do This
- **Leading:** "You don't like X, right?"
- **Multiple:** "What and why and how?"
- **Assumption:** "You probably find X difficult?"
- **Closed:** "Do you use X?" (save for screener)
- **Jargon:** Use participant language

### Do This Instead
- **Neutral:** "How do you feel about X?"
- **Single:** "What do you do?"
- **Open:** "How does X work for you?"
- **Open-ended:** "Tell me about your experience with X"
- **Plain:** "How would you describe X?"
