# Product Vision

Define the foundational statements that guide your product decisions. A strong product vision aligns teams, attracts the right customers, and provides decision-making clarity.

## When to Use This Skill

Use when:
- Starting a new product and need to articulate the vision
- Pivoting and need to refine the vision
- Onboarding team members who need to understand the product direction
- Making strategic decisions and need a north star
- Preparing for investor conversations

## Workflow

### Step 1: Gather Context

Before defining vision elements, understand:

1. **What problem does your product solve?**
2. **Who experiences this problem?**
3. **What is your current solution?**
4. **What makes your approach different?**
5. **What does success look like?**

If context is limited, focus on what is known and note gaps.

### Step 2: Draft Vision Statement

The vision answers: Where are you going? What future are you creating?

A good vision is:
- Aspirational but achievable
- 3-5 years out
- Inspires the team
- Differentiates from competitors

### Step 3: Draft Mission Statement

The mission answers: Why do we exist? What is our purpose?

A good mission is:
- 1-2 sentences
- Customer-focused
- Action-oriented

### Step 4: Define Positioning

Positioning answers: How do we win? For whom?

### Step 5: Establish Core Values

The principles that guide decisions.

### Step 6: Test Your Vision

Use the checklist in references to validate quality.

## Output Format

The skill produces these sections:

**PRODUCT VISION**

Vision statement (3-5 year aspiration)

**MISSION STATEMENT**

Mission (why you exist)

**POSITIONING STATEMENT**

For [target customer] who [need/problem], [product name] is [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [competitor], [key differentiator].

**CORE VALUES**

Value 1: Description

Value 3: Description

**STRATEGIC PILLARS**

Pillar 1: Description
Pillar 2: Description
Pillar 3: Description

**SUCCESS INDICATORS**
