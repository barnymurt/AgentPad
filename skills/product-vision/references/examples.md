# Real-World Product Vision Examples

## How These Companies Evolved Their Vision

### Stripe

**Early:** "We make it easy to accept payments online"

**Evolution:** "Increase the GDP of the internet"

**What we can learn:** Start concrete, then expand to impact.

**Current Vision:** Stripe's vision evolved from "easy payments" to "economic infrastructure for the internet." They recognized that the problem wasn't payments, it was enabling commerce online at scale.

**Takeaway:** Your vision can evolve. Start where you understand, expand as you learn.

---

### Airbnb

**Early:** "Book rooms with locals"

**Evolution:** "Belong anywhere"

**What we can learn:** From transactions to transformation.

**Current Vision:** Airbnb moved from a room-booking service to belonging anywhere in the world. They realized the core value wasn't the room, it was the experience of being somewhere new.

**Takeaway:** Dig deeper than the feature to find the emotional core.

---

### Slack

**Early:** "Email is broken, let's fix it"

**Evolution:** "Your operating system for work"

**What we can learn:** From pain point to platform.

**Current Vision:** Slack started as "email killer" but evolved to being the hub for workplace communication and eventually integrated with nearly every work tool.

**Takeaway:** The problem you solve is often bigger than your initial framing.

---

### Shopify

**Easy to sell online"

**Evolution:** "Make commerce better for everyone"

**What we can learn:** From ease to empowerment.

**Current Vision:** Shopify started with "make it easy to sell online" for small merchants. Now powers large enterprises too. The vision expanded from ease to democratizing commerce.

**Takeaway:** Your vision should stretch as you grow.

---

### Notion

**All-in-one workspace"

**Evolution:** "Make it possible for every person to shape their work and life"

**What we can learn:** From tool to transformation.

**Current Vision:** Notion moved from "workspace for notes and docs" to shaping work and life. A much bolder, more inspiring statement.

**Takeaway:** Connect to larger purpose.

## Anti-Patterns: What Bad Vision Looks Like

### Example 1: Too Generic

"We want to create innovative solutions that delight our customers and make a positive impact in the world."

Issues:
- Could be any company
- No specific problem addressed
- No differentiation
- Not actionable

### Example 2: Too Product-Focused

"Our platform uses AI and machine learning to provide real-time analytics dashboards that help businesses make data-driven decisions."

Issues:
- Describes features, not value
- Technical rather than customer-focused
- Could be 100 other products

### Example 3: Too Narrow

"We help small businesses in Ohio create invoices in under 5 minutes."

Issues:
- Too specific to be a vision
- Limits growth potential
- No emotional connection

### Example 4: Too Complex

"Our vision is to leverage cutting-edge technologies, foster strategic partnerships, and build an ecosystem that democratizes access to enterprise-grade capabilities while maintaining sustainability and social responsibility."

Issues:
- Impossible to remember
- No clear direction
- Tries to be everything

---

## Good Vision Statements

**Stripe:** Increase the GDP of the internet

**Airbnb:** Belong anywhere

**Shopify:** Make commerce better for everyone

**Spotify:** A music service for everyone

**Notion:** Make it possible for every person to shape their work and life

**Common traits:**
- Short (3-7 words)
- Memorable
- Customer-focused
- Ambitious but clear

## Positioning Statement Examples

### Stripe
"For businesses who need to accept payments online, Stripe is a payments platform that handles the complexity so you can focus on your business. Unlike PayPal, Stripe integrates directly into your product."

### Slack
"For teams who need to communicate effectively, Slack is a communication tool that brings all your conversations into one place. Unlike email, Slack is designed for real-time collaboration."

### Shopify
"For entrepreneurs who want to sell online, Shopify is an e-commerce platform that provides everything you need to start and grow your business. Unlike building your own store, Shopify handles the technical complexity."
