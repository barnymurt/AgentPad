# Vision Quality Checklist

Use these questions to test if your vision is strong.

---

## Clarity Test

Can you explain your vision in one sentence?

Can someone understand it without knowing your product?

Can a customer understand the benefit without technical knowledge?

---

## Inspiration Test

Does it make you excited to work on?

Does it attract the customers you want?

Would someone want to tell their friends about it?

---

## Differentiation Test

Could your competitor say the same thing?

What makes your vision unique?

Is there something specific only you can achieve?

---

## Feasibility Test

Is this actually achievable in 3-5 years?

Do you have a path to get there?

Are there milestones along the way?

---

## Focus Test

Does it help you say no to things that don't matter?

Does it clarify priorities?

Would different team members make the same decisions?

---

## Customer Focus Test

Is the customer the hero, or is the product?

Does it solve a real problem?

Would a customer care, or is it internally focused?

---

## Quick Validation Questions

Answer these honestly:

1. Is your vision shorter than a tweet?

2. Could you put it on a t-shirt?

3. Does it make sense to a 10-year-old?

4. Would your mother understand it?

5. Is there a specific problem or benefit mentioned?

---

## If You Answered "No" to Most

Your vision may need work. Consider:

- Start with what makes you different
- Focus on the problem, not the solution
- Make it personal and emotional
- Test with real potential customers
