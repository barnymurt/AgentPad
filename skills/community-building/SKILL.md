# Community Building

Build a community around your product to drive engagement and growth. This skill creates a community strategy.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Define Community Goals
- What is the community for?
- Who should join?
- What's the value?

### Step 2: Select Platform
- Slack, Discord, Circle, forum
- Match to audience

### Step 3: Engagement Strategy
- Content plan
- Events
- Member activities

### Step 4: Growth Plan
- Invitation strategy
- Ambassador program
- Viral loops

## Output Format

The output follows the structure defined in [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md):

- **Section 1:** Goals & Vision
- **Section 2:** Platform Strategy
- **Section 3:** Engagement Plan
- **Section 4:** Growth Strategy

## Quality Criteria

- [ ] Goals are clear
- [ ] Platform matches audience
- [ ] Engagement is sustainable

## Common Mistakes

1. **No clear value** — Why should people join?
2. **No moderation** — Community goes off track.
3. **Scaling too fast** — Quality suffers.
